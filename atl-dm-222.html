The Artist

Eight Years,
One Standard.

Based out of Atlanta's Old Fourth Ward, ATL DM 222 has spent eight years building a reputation on precision, storytelling, and work that ages well. No flash-in-the-pan trends — just clean lines, solid blacks, and pieces built to last a lifetime.

Trained under two Old School masters before carving out an independent style that bridges traditional Americana with contemporary fine-line and blackwork. Every booking is a collaboration — your story, rendered permanently.

Walk-ins welcome when available. Commissioned and custom work by appointment. Flash goes fast — book early.
